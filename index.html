WHY MIHAYO'S

Guided by people who never left

Every guide on our roster grew up within a day's drive of the parks they lead in. That's not a marketing line — it's how we staff trips.

Local Ownership

Tanzanian-owned and operated. Profits stay in the communities bordering the parks, not offshore.

Conservation-Linked

A portion of every booking funds anti-poaching patrols and Maasai-led conservancy programs.

Small Groups, Real Vehicles

Modified Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs, max 6 guests per vehicle — never a convoy of vans.

Flexible Ground Team

Weather shifts, roads wash out, migrations move early. Our team re-routes same-day, no call center.

Climb Kilimanjaro

Lemosho

Longer approach through pristine forest, the highest summit success rate. From $2,650.
